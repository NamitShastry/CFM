Privacy Policy

Privacy & Trust

Your privacy and trust are important to us. Any personal information you provide—including your name, email address, contact details, uploaded files, and project information—is used solely for communicating with you and completing your project.

All client files, ideas, and project details are treated as confidential and will never be shared with third parties without your permission, unless required by applicable law. We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, while giving material we expect that you will not violate any copyright laws; you are fully responsible for the content.

Usage Rights

Unless otherwise agreed, all final deliverables are supplied without our branding, allowing you to use them for personal or commercial purposes. Once delivered, you are free to present the work as part of your brand.

If a project includes third-party assets (fonts, music, etc.), their use remains subject to the original creator's license. We will use your name in our client list unless you expressly ask us not to.
